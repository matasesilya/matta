About Me
I studied Monumental Plastic Arts, a discipline that involved working with a wide variety of materials. Since childhood, every material has been a source of inspiration for me — a means of creating something and expressing my own emotions.
Through various techniques, ranging from sculpting and mural painting to weaving objects from bamboo, window display design, ceramics, textile painting and felting, I have developed a personal relationship with each material.
I find the direct collaboration with a material, translating my inner world through its inherent nature and transforming it into a tangible object through the sublimation of my libido, to be a deeply fascinating process.
Through this process, I become increasingly aware of how people perceive and interpret my work, which allows me to experience the significance of my own actions on an even deeper level
